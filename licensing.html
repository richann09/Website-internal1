Motorcyle Licensing in NZ

Understanding the pathway from learner to full license.

Learner License

The learner license is the first stage of motorcycle licensing. Riders must complete a theory test and follow learner license conditions while gaining riding experience.

Restricted License

The restricted license allows riders to gain further road experience while following license restrictions designed to improve safety.

Full license

Riders can apply for a full license after meeting the required conditions and completing the appropriate tests or approved training courses.
