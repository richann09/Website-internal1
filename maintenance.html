Motorcycle Maintenance

Basic maintenance helps keep your motorcycle safe and reliable.

Pre-Ride Checks

Before every ride, riders should check tyres, lights, indicators, fuel levels and brakes.

Chain Maintenance

A motorcycle chain should be cleaned and lubricated regularly to improve performance and reduce wear.

Tyre Pressure

Riders should regularly check tyre pressure and tread depth to maintain safety and handling performance.

Maintenance Checklist

  • Check tyre pressure
  • Inspect chain condition
  • Test lights and indicators
  • Check oil level
  • Inspect brakes
