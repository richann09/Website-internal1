Types of Motorcycles

Different motorcycles are designed for different riding styles and purposes.

Sport Bikes

Designed for performance, speed and agile handling.

Naked Bikes

Versatile motorcycles suited for everyday riding.

Cruisers

Comfortable motorcycles with a relaxed riding position.

Adventure Bikes

Built for long-distance touring and mixed road conditions.

Which Bike Is Best For You?

New riders often choose naked bikes because they are practical, comfortable and beginner-friendly. More experienced riders may prefer sport bikes or adventure bikes depending on their needs.
