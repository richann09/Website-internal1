Motorcycle safety

Learn safe riding habits and protective gear for riding in NZ.

Protective Gear

Wearing the correct safety gear can help reduce injuries if an accident occurs. Riders should wear a helmet, gloves, riding jacket, pants, and sturdy footwear.

Stay Visible

Riders should wear bright or reflective clothing and use headlights during the day to improve visbility to other road users.

Safe riding habits

  • Maintain a safe following distance.
  • Check mirrors regularly.
  • Ride to road conditions.
  • Stay alert and avoid distractions.
